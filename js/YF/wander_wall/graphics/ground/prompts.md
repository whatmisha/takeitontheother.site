## 01_lime_fur.png

Референс: `letters/lime fur/_ref.jpg`

Финальный промпт:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: isolated 3D terrain surface for a graphic design key visual, onto which the designer will later place separate objects.
Primary request: Create ONE wide horizontal landscape / ground patch made entirely from the material of the supplied reference. This is an empty usable ground surface, fully isolated on a genuinely transparent background with an actual alpha channel.
Input image 1: material, texture, palette and rendering-style reference only; do not copy its object shape or background.
Composition: landscape canvas, approximately 3:2 aspect ratio. A single broad continuous organically shaped ground patch viewed from the front and slightly above, camera elevation around 25 degrees, with the upper surface clearly visible and generous depth for placing objects. A wide gently undulating central plateau occupying most of the surface; low rolling contours toward the sides and rear. Terrain is visually broad and low, with a shallow irregular perimeter rather than a tall pedestal. Entire terrain silhouette visible with comfortable transparent margins on every side, no cropping. No detached pieces.
Lighting: polished tactile studio 3D rendering in the spirit of the reference, broad soft key from upper left, gentle dimensional self-shadowing, all surface detail sharp. Shadows only within the object; no external ground plane or cast shadow on a backdrop.
Constraints: genuinely transparent empty surroundings; no white/gray background, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no horizon, no sky, no typography, no letters, no symbols, no decorative objects or products. Preserve the distinctive reference material rather than converting it into generic rock or grass.
Materials/textures: Match the supplied reference's vivid acidic yellow-lime green, extremely dense long fine silky fur, individual wispy hairs and richly shaded green valleys. Cover the entire continuous ground patch in this same luxurious soft fur. Shape it into broad low furry rolling hills surrounding a generous shallow central meadow-like plateau, soft slightly tufted contours and a naturally fuzzy silhouette. It must read as a surreal terrain made of the reference's fur, with flowing directional strands, not real grass, not a flower, not a ring and not multiple separate pillows. Preserve the luminous lime highlights and darker saturated green within the fur.
```
