How it Works?

Click Add Image to add an image for processing.

Click Add Layout to upload a transparent PNG with your layout mockup. This will help you scale the texture according to your layout.

By default, the texture is exported at 600 pixels on the larger side with a transparent background.

You can save a non-transparent PNG with a background of your chosen color by selecting it in the Background Color setting and unchecking the Export with Transparency checkbox.

The image can be exported at ×2, ×4, and ×8 resolutions, but keep in mind that processing settings will be applied to the enlarged image — for example, if Pixel Size is set to 1, it will also be 1 in the final enlarged image (not 2, 4, or 8).

Both Texture Transform and Texture Settings panels can be freely moved around the screen by dragging their headers.
